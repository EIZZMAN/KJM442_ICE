1. Fundamental Concepts

Otto Cycle vs Diesel Cycle

The primary difference lies in the Heat Addition process. In an Otto cycle (Petrol), heat is added at a constant volume. In a Diesel cycle, heat is added at a constant pressure.

Compression Ratio ($r$)

Defined as the ratio of the maximum volume to the minimum volume in the cylinder. Diesel engines require higher ratios ($r \approx 14-22$) to achieve auto-ignition compared to Otto engines ($r \approx 7-10$).

1. P-V Diagram Comparison

Understanding the Curves

In the P-V diagram, the area enclosed by the cycle represents the Net Work Done. For Electrical students, think of this as the "Power Output" before it reaches the generator.

  • Otto Cycle: Features a vertical line for heat addition (Constant Volume).
  • Diesel Cycle: Features a horizontal line for heat addition (Constant Pressure).

Thermodynamic Cycles

The Otto cycle adds heat at Constant Volume ($V=C$). The Diesel cycle adds heat at Constant Pressure ($P=C$).

Compression Ratio ($r$)

Efficiency is calculated as: $$\eta = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{k-1}}$$ Increasing the ratio $r$ improves performance but may lead to engine knocking.

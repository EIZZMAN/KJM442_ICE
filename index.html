1. Study Notes: Thermodynamic Cycles

Cycle Comparison: Otto vs. Diesel

The primary distinction between these two cycles is the Heat Addition process (Process $2 \rightarrow 3$).

Key Feature Otto Cycle (Petrol/SI) Diesel Cycle (CI)
Heat Addition Constant Volume ($V = C$) Constant Pressure ($P = C$)
Compression Ratio ($r$) Moderate ($r \approx 7-10$) High ($r \approx 14-22$)
Efficiency ($\eta$) $\eta_{otto} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{k-1}}$ $\eta_{diesel} = 1 - \frac{1}{r^{k-1}} \left[ \frac{\rho^k - 1}{k(\rho - 1)} \right]$
Otto Cycle P-V Diagram
  • 1 $\rightarrow$ 2: Isentropic Compression
  • 2 $\rightarrow$ 3: Constant Volume Heat Addition
  • 3 $\rightarrow$ 4: Isentropic Expansion
  • 4 $\rightarrow$ 1: Constant Volume Heat Rejection
Diesel Cycle P-V Diagram
  • 1 $\rightarrow$ 2: Isentropic Compression
  • 2 $\rightarrow$ 3: Constant Pressure Heat Addition
  • 3 $\rightarrow$ 4: Isentropic Expansion
  • 4 $\rightarrow$ 1: Constant Volume Heat Rejection
